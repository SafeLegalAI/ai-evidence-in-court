# Agent brief — AI-generated evidence & deepfakes in court (read AGENT-BRIEF-COMMON.md first, then both schemas)

**Table `decisions`.** One row per ruling, any jurisdiction, in which (i) allegedly AI-generated, AI-altered or AI-"enhanced" material (video, audio, images, documents, screenshots, expert reports, witness or victim statements, chatbot output) was offered as evidence and its treatment decided; (ii) a party attacked genuine-looking evidence as a deepfake ("deepfake defence" / liar's dividend); or (iii) a party tendered acknowledged AI output (a ChatGPT answer, an AI "expert" analysis) and the court ruled on its weight. **Not** fabricated-citation sanctions (those are the incident tracker) — but if one decision does both, include it here for the evidence ruling and set `related_opinion_id` if it is also in `us-genai-court-opinions`.

Code from the text: `evidence_type`, `ai_role`, `offered_by`, `challenge`, `ruling`, `rules_cited` (FRE 901/902/702/403, state equivalents, Canada Evidence Act s.31.1, Indian Evidence Act/BSA s.63, CPR/PACE…). `court_finding_quote`: the court's own words on the AI question — up to 120 words for US (public domain, `text_licence: public-domain`), ≤25 words for everything else (`quoted-short`). `sensitive: true` for children, sexual offences, mental health, domestic abuse — then keep the summary to the ruling and use the court's own anonymisation. `publication_ban_checked: true` only after you looked for a ban/anonymity order (CanLII headers, Australian suppression notes, UK reporting restrictions). Never link or describe the alleged media itself.

**Sources by jurisdiction.** US: court sites, nycourts.gov/reporter, govinfo, RECAP PDFs (see common brief). Canada: CanLII is **human-read only** — open the page yourself, read, quote ≤25 words, link; no scripted fetch of canlii.org. Australia: AustLII likewise (link the official court/AustLII URL). UK: Find Case Law — human-read, link. India/Italy/Israel/others: court sites; if only a news report exists, `verification: link-only` and say so. Leads: Damien Charlotin's CC0 databases https://www.damiencharlotin.com/deepfakes/ (20 cases, CSV) and https://www.damiencharlotin.com/ai-evidence/ (34 cases, CSV) — download the CSVs, treat every row as a lead, re-read the primary decision; the FJC deepfake survey (May 2026 Evidence Rules agenda book, pp. ~226 ff.) for federal dockets; Matthew Lee's International Deepfake Case Law Database (lead only); CourtListener discovery search `q="deepfake" OR "deep fake" OR "AI-enhanced" OR "AI-generated"` with `authenticat*`.

**Table `instruments`.** One row per rule/proposal/statute/bench card/guidance/survey/report on AI-generated or machine-generated evidence. Must include with exact current status quoted from uscourts.gov materials: **proposed FRE 707** (published Aug 2025; comment period closed 2026-02-16; Advisory Committee on Evidence Rules meeting 2026-05-07 — did not advance; quote the agenda book; `next_event` = the next meeting/mini-conference); the studied **draft FRE 901(c)**; FRE 104/902 August 2026 preliminary draft (only if AI-relevant — check the PDF); **Louisiana Act 250 (HB 178, 2025)** CCP arts 371(C), 1551; the FJC judges' survey (2026-03-25); NCSC/TRI bench cards on acknowledged and unacknowledged AI evidence; California Judicial Council AI Task Force evidence work; UK Judiciary AI guidance Oct 2025 deepfake section; any state evidence-rule amendments or court forms about AI-generated exhibits; Canada/Australia/Singapore/India equivalents if they exist. `operative_text` verbatim for public-domain/OGL instruments, ≤25 words otherwise. `status_quote` ≤25 words from the source that establishes the status.

`decision_id`: `<country>-<court-slug>-<yyyy>-<case-slug>` e.g. `us-wa-king-superior-2024-state-v-puloka`, `ca-abkb-2026-r-v-flett`. `instrument_id`: e.g. `us-fre-707-proposed`, `us-la-act-250-2025`, `uk-judiciary-ai-guidance-2025-deepfakes`.
